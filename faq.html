Sizing & what it powers

System size is decided by what you want to run and for how long, not by the size of the building. As a practical guide, based on our published packages:

  • 3–5 kVA: lights, fans, TVs, one fridge/freezer, sometimes one 1 HP air conditioner
  • 8–10 kW: a full family home with 2 fridges, 2 freezers and two to three 1.5 HP ACs
  • 12–16 kW: a large home or duplex with three to five 1.5 HP ACs and a pumping machine
  • 20–30 kW: an estate residence, office or small commercial building, often three-phase
  • 50 kW and above: hotels, schools, hospitals, warehouses and industrial facilities

Air conditioning is almost always the deciding factor. Our engineers carry out a free load assessment before quoting, so the system is sized against your actual appliances rather than a guess. You can start with our solar calculator.

A 1.5 HP inverter air conditioner draws roughly 1.1–1.3 kW while running. Running one for a normal day, with battery to cover the night, needs roughly four to six 620 W panels dedicated to that load once losses are accounted for.

In practice we never size panels per appliance; we size the array against total daily energy use in kWh and the battery against overnight demand. For reference, our 16 kW package (20 × 620 W panels and 2 × 16 kWh batteries) is rated for five 1.5 HP air conditioners alongside a full household load.

Yes. Every one of our packages from 5 kVA upward is rated to run air conditioning, and our larger systems run multiple units continuously.

Two things make it work: a hybrid inverter with enough capacity to absorb the compressor's starting surge, and enough lithium storage to carry the AC through the night. This is why we size against the specific AC units on site, a 3 HP unit and a 1 HP unit place very different demands on the same system.

A 16 kW / 48 V system with two 16 kWh lithium batteries and 20 × 620 W panels is rated for 70 bulbs, 10 fans, 4 fridges and freezers, a washing machine, five 1.5 HP air conditioners, a pumping machine, a microwave and an electric blender.

We have installed exactly this configuration for a two-bedroom short-stay apartment running three air conditioners (two 1.5 HP and one 3 HP) plus refrigeration, television and lighting. See the full case study.

Yes. That is the point of a battery-based system. Our installations use hybrid inverters with lithium battery storage, so when the grid fails the system continues supplying your building from stored energy and from the panels.

There is no transfer delay and no switchover gap, which is why sensitive equipment such as medical refrigeration, ICT and POS systems stay online through an outage.

Yes. Solar panels generate from daylight, not direct sunshine, so they continue producing on cloudy days, at reduced output.

This is why battery capacity matters as much as panel count in Nigeria. We size the storage so that a run of overcast days is carried by the battery bank rather than forcing you back onto a generator, and we oversize arrays relative to storage so the bank refills within a single average solar day.

Installation & process

Installation is completed within 48 hours of payment confirmation for most systems, except in rare cases of unforeseen circumstances or delays. Larger commercial and industrial systems typically take one to three working days.

The full process is: consultation, personalised proposal, site visit where necessary, payment and agreement, scheduling and installation, testing and commissioning, then after-sales support.

  • 2. Proposal: a personalised quotation with recommended system size, cost breakdown, warranty terms and payment plan.

Equipment, warranty & maintenance

On our Deye and Solis product lines: 25 years on solar panels, 10 years on lithium batteries, 5 years on inverters, plus 1 year of free after-sales service support.

On our standard product line: 20 years on solar panels, 5 years on lithium batteries, 2 years on inverters, 1 year on SMF/tubular batteries, 1 year on charge controllers and 1 year of free after-sales support.

Solar panels typically last 25 to 30 years, with warranties guaranteeing performance for at least 20 to 25 years.

Battery life depends on the chemistry. Lithium batteries last 10 to 15 years. Drycell and tubular batteries last 2 to 3 years, which is why we specify lithium on virtually every system we install, the higher upfront cost is recovered several times over across the life of the installation.

Very little. Regular cleaning of the panels so they are not obstructed by dust or dirt, plus occasional checks, is usually all that is required.

Every installation includes one year of free after-sales service support, and we carry out regular system health checks. There are no filters to change, no oil to top up and no fuel to buy.

A complete solar system has five parts:

  • Solar panels: photovoltaic modules that capture sunlight and convert it into direct current (DC) electricity.
  • Inverter: converts DC electricity into the alternating current (AC) your appliances use.
  • Battery storage: stores excess energy for use at night or on cloudy days.
  • MPPT charge controller: regulates voltage and current from the panels to prevent battery overcharging.
  • Mounting structures: secure the panels in place at the optimum angle for sunlight.

Buying an inverter alone gives you backup. Buying the full system gives you generation.

We supply and install Deye, Solis and ALP Solar hybrid inverters, alongside Bauman Energy, BICODI and SRNE equipment, with lithium battery storage from tier-1 manufacturers.

We publish separate price charts for our Deye and Solis product lines so you can compare configurations and pricing directly on our pricing page.

Yes. Alongside solar panels, hybrid inverters, lithium batteries and MPPT charge controllers we supply and install solar water heaters, submersible pumps, inverter air conditioners and solar street lighting systems.

All are installed by our own teams and covered by the same after-sales support.
