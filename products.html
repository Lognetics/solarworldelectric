Quick answer

What products does Solar World Electric sell? Monocrystalline solar panels (620 W and 460 W), hybrid solar inverters from Deye, Solis and ALP Solar, lithium battery storage from 2.5 kWh to 16 kWh per module, MPPT charge controllers and mounting structures, plus solar water heaters, submersible pumps, inverter air conditioners and all-in-one solar street lights. Everything is supplied installed and commissioned by our own teams.

Components

What we supply and install

Monocrystalline solar panels

620 W and 460 W monocrystalline modules, the panels behind every array we install, carrying up to a 25-year warranty.

Hybrid solar inverters

Deye, Solis and ALP Solar hybrid inverters from 3 kVA to 125 kW, single-phase and three-phase, with 5-year warranty on our Deye and Solis lines.

Lithium battery storage

Modules from 2.5 kWh to 16 kWh, stackable into racks with battery management. 10-year warranty and a 10–15 year service life.

MPPT charge controllers

Maximum power point tracking controllers that regulate charge and protect the battery bank from overcharging.

Solar street lighting

All-in-one units with integrated PV, lithium battery, controller and LED head. Automatic dusk-to-dawn operation, no grid connection.

Pumps, heaters & inverter ACs

Solar submersible pumps, solar water heaters and inverter air conditioners, supplied and installed alongside your system.
