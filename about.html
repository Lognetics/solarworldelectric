Who We Are

A trusted leader in Nigeria's renewable energy sector

At Solar World Electric Technology Limited, we are committed to transforming the way homes, businesses and industries access electricity through innovative, sustainable and cost-effective solar energy solutions.

We specialise in the sales, installation, financing and maintenance of high-quality solar power systems designed to meet the unique energy demands of residential, commercial and industrial customers. Our team of experienced professionals delivers world-class installations, exceptional customer service and long-term energy solutions that empower clients to achieve true energy independence.

Our Vision

To become the leading provider of solar and renewable energy solutions in Nigeria and across Africa — delivering reliable, sustainable and affordable power to homes, businesses and industries.
