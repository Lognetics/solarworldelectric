Who We Are

From Sunlight to Self-Reliance — clean energy you can rely on.

Solar World Electric Technology Limited is a trusted leader in Nigeria's renewable energy sector, specialising in the sales, installation, financing and maintenance of high-quality solar power systems for residential, commercial and industrial customers.
