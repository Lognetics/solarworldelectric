The real comparison

A generator is cheap to buy and expensive to own

A generator's purchase price is the smallest number in the story. Fuel, servicing, parts and replacement never stop, and they rise every year.

A solar system inverts that shape. The cost is concentrated once, at the start, and generation afterwards is effectively free. Our panels carry a 25-year warranty and our lithium batteries a 10-year warranty, so the system keeps producing long after a generator bought on the same day would have been replaced twice.

Our process

From first conversation to switch-on

Seven steps, no surprises. Most installations are completed within 48 hours of payment confirmation.

Start with a free consultation

Consultation

We start with a conversation to understand your energy needs, your current challenges and your budget. This helps us propose the right solution for you.

Proposal

Based on our discussion we send a personalised quotation including recommended system size, cost breakdown, warranty terms and conditions, and payment plan.

Site visit

Where necessary we schedule a physical inspection to check your wiring, roof strength and energy load, confirming the proposal is technically suitable for your space.

Payment & agreement

Once you have read and agreed our terms of delivery and service, payment is made to a company account and a receipt is issued. We then prepare for delivery and installation.

Scheduling & installation

Our team schedules your installation and carries it out on the agreed day, typically within 1–3 working days depending on system size.

Testing & commissioning

After installation we test the system thoroughly and show you how to use it, and give you a guide so you always know what to do.

After-sales support

Even after installation we stay with you. You enjoy warranty coverage, priority support and regular system health checks from our team.

Questions people ask us

Solar in Nigeria, answered plainly

The questions we are asked most often, with straight answers, real prices and no hedging.

A complete solar and inverter system from Solar World Electric Technology Ltd. ranges from about ₦1.49 million for a 3 kVA inverter package up to ₦147 million for a 125 kW industrial system. Typical Nigerian homes fall between ₦3.5 million and ₦16 million installed, depending on how many air conditioners and refrigeration units the system has to carry.

Prices are quoted in two parts, the inverter package (inverter, lithium battery, cables, accessories and installation) and the solar package (the panel array, cables, accessories and installation). Many customers buy the inverter package first and add the solar array later. Our full published price charts are on the pricing page.

A 5 kVA / 48 V inverter package with one 5 kWh lithium battery, cables, accessories and installation is ₦2,820,000. The matching 4-panel 620 W solar package is ₦1,120,000, making a complete 5 kVA solar and inverter system approximately ₦3,940,000 installed.

A larger-storage version using a 15.33 kWh lithium battery is ₦4,300,000 for the inverter package and ₦1,880,000 for an 8-panel array. A 5 kVA system is rated for around 35 bulbs, 6 fans, 4 television sets, a standard fridge, a freezer, a washing machine, one 1 HP air conditioner and an electric blender.

A 10 kW / 48 V Deye inverter package with a 16 kWh lithium battery is ₦7,000,000, and the matching 8-panel 620 W solar package is ₦2,290,000: approximately ₦9,290,000 for the complete installed system.

A 10 kW system is rated for 50 bulbs, 10 fans, 6 television sets, 2 fridges, 2 freezers, a washing machine, three 1.5 HP air conditioners, a pumping machine, microwave and electric blender. It suits a duplex or a large family home.

Over the life of the system, yes. A generator has a lower purchase price but an unlimited running cost, fuel, servicing, parts and eventual replacement, and it only produces power while you are paying for fuel.

A solar system is the opposite: the cost is concentrated upfront, and generation afterwards is effectively free. Our panels carry a 25-year warranty and our lithium batteries a 10-year warranty, so the system keeps producing long after a generator bought at the same time would have been replaced twice.

There are also costs a generator carries that never appear on an invoice: noise, fumes, the maintenance burden and the risk of being without power when fuel is unavailable.

System size is decided by what you want to run and for how long, not by the size of the building. As a practical guide, based on our published packages:

  • 3–5 kVA: lights, fans, TVs, one fridge/freezer, sometimes one 1 HP air conditioner
  • 8–10 kW: a full family home with 2 fridges, 2 freezers and two to three 1.5 HP ACs
  • 12–16 kW: a large home or duplex with three to five 1.5 HP ACs and a pumping machine
  • 20–30 kW: an estate residence, office or small commercial building, often three-phase
  • 50 kW and above: hotels, schools, hospitals, warehouses and industrial facilities

Air conditioning is almost always the deciding factor. Our engineers carry out a free load assessment before quoting, so the system is sized against your actual appliances rather than a guess. You can start with our solar calculator.
