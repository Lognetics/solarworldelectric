How sizing actually works

Inverter size is set by your peak simultaneous demand — mostly air-conditioner start-up surge. Battery capacity is set by your overnight energy use in kWh. Panel count is set by how fast you need the battery refilled the next day. Getting one right and the others wrong is why undersized systems fail at 3 a.m.
